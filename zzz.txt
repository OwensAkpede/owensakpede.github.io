# his dog

The dog's name was Max. He had a coat the color of morning sun and eyes that held the wisdom of a creature far older than his seven years. Max was more than just a loyal friend; he was a silent guardian. He seemed to understand John's moods better than anyone. A sad day meant a gentle head resting on John's lap. A happy day was met with ecstatic tail wags and a playful bark that echoed through their small, cozy home. Max had a peculiar habit of "talking," a series of soft rumbles and growls that John swore he could understand perfectly. Their conversations were a secret language, a bond forged in silent understanding and shared moments.
